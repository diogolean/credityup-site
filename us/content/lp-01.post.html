What is your primary goal today?

Most dating apps are not hard because of one clever feature. They are hard because the first ten minutes are a run of screens that never say what happens next.

This page is a map of the simpler ones. It will not name a winner, and it will not tell you a match is likely. It shows what to check before you create an account, then points you at a short guide on this same site.

A short profile is a feature

If an app asks for a long biography before you can see anyone, that is a product choice, not a test of character. Look for a flow you can finish in one sitting: a few photos, an age range, a city, and one honest line about what you want. You can add more later.

Filters should narrow the search, not hide the price

Age, distance, and the kind of relationship you are open to are enough for a first pass. A wall of extra filters that only opens after a payment is a reason to read the price screen slowly. Recurring charges should be visible before you confirm them. Google Play's subscription notes are a useful checklist for that.

See who can view you

Before you upload a face photo, open the privacy settings. You want a plain answer to three questions: who can see the photo, whether you appear in search, and how to pause the profile without deleting it. If those controls are hard to find, treat that as part of the decision.

The first message should be easy to find

A usable app puts the conversation on the same screen as the match, with an ordinary text field. You do not need a perfect opening. One specific detail from the profile is enough. If the only way to talk is a paid sticker or a timed game, decide whether that is the conversation you wanted.

Meeting up

If you do meet, keep the first meeting in a public place, tell a friend where you are going, and use your own way home. Do not send money, gift cards, or identity documents to someone you have not met. The FTC's notes on romance scams are short and worth reading.

Where the buttons go

The three buttons under the quiz stay on us.credityup.com. One opens the shorter beginner guide, one opens practical tips, and one opens the longer walkthrough. None of them leave this domain, and none of them create an account for you.

Conditions vary by app and by state. Check the app's own rules and the store listing before you pay for anything.
