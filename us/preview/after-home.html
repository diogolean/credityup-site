Before you meet someone

Don't send money

Gift cards, crypto, and “travel emergencies” are the usual romance-scam ask. Stop the conversation there.

First meeting in public

Tell a friend where you are going and use your own way home. You can leave.

Keep the photo small

Check who can see your profile before you upload a face. Skip your home address and daily route.
