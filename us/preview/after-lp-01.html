What is your primary goal today?

Most of the easy dating apps in the United States let you start on a free plan. The paid decision shows up later, as a premium subscription. The names people compare are Tinder Gold, Bumble Premium, and Hinge's paid plans (Hinge+ and HingeX).

This page is about what those upgrades usually add, and what you can still do without them. It does not rank a winner, it does not start a subscription, and it does not promise a match. The guides linked below stay on this site.

What the free plan usually includes

On a free plan you can typically create a profile, set an age range and a distance, and send a limited number of likes or introductions. Use that week before you pay. It tells you whether people nearby are active, and whether the app is actually easy to finish a profile in one sitting: a few photos, a city, and one honest line about what you want.

Tinder Gold, Bumble Premium, and Hinge's paid plans

These are the premium subscriptions US advertisers and app stores put next to the free tier. Tinder Gold is the Tinder plan built around seeing who already liked you, on top of a fast deck of profiles. Bumble Premium sits on Bumble's free messaging rules and usually adds more filters and more control over who can reach you. Hinge's paid plans, Hinge+ and HingeX, sit on top of an app that asks for prompts instead of a one-line bio. Plan names change. The current name is on the product page inside the app.

What the paid plan usually sells is the same short list: more likes, a look at who already liked you, extra filters, and a boost that shows your profile to more people for a short time. The monthly price and the renewal date are on the Google Play or App Store listing, and again on the confirm screen. They are not the same in every state or every promotion. Read that screen. A review that quotes a dollar amount is already out of date.

Compare the extra, not the badge

A useful comparison is one question: which single extra would change how you use the free plan you already tried? If you would not pay for "see who liked you", unlimited likes, or a boost, the premium subscription is optional. None of these plans is a match guarantee, and a paid badge is not a sign that someone is a better fit.

Verification is not included just because you paid

Photo verification and phone verification are separate from the premium tier. Check whether the app offers them, who can see a verified photo, and how to pause the profile without deleting it. A subscription does not make a profile honest. Before a first meeting, pick a public place, use your own way home, and do not send money, gift cards, or identity documents to someone you have not met.
